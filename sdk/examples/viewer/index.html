Two kinds of read-only

They are not the same thing, and treating them as one produces both of the bad outcomes: a viewer that reads as a broken editor, and a thumbnail that invites clicking on things it will refuse.

access: "view"

An access level. This person is working in the sheet and may not change it, so they get the whole application minus the commands that write — scroll, select, navigate sheets, zoom, copy, find, export, print, recalculate. Open the menus: Format, Insert and Data are gone; File keeps Download and Print. Nothing is greyed out, because a menu full of things you can never enable is worse than a shorter menu.

access: "preview"

A presentation. A row in a file list, an attachment rendered inline. No chrome at all and nothing suggesting there is work to do here — though a range can still be selected and copied, because refusing that costs the reader something and protects nothing.
