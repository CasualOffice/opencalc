OpenCalc

A spreadsheet you can co-edit. This page is the demo host — the part a product supplies when it integrates.

Open a document, press Share, and send the link to somebody else. You will both be editing the same sheet, and either of you can download it afterwards.

Reading this to decide whether to integrate? The whole contract is server/casual-calc-host/src/main.rs — four endpoints and a signed token. Your product replaces this host; the collaboration server and the editor stay as they are.
